Q1: "Kenapa ibu rumah tangga mau pakai DapurKu, bukan GoFood?"
A: Tiga alasan utama:
   • Komisi: GoFood 20-30% untuk restoran. DapurKu 10-15% untuk rumahan.
     Dari Rp 40.000 pesanan: GoFood ambil Rp 8.000-12.000, DapurKu ambil Rp 4.800-6.000.
     Penjual dapat Rp 10.000-15.000 lebih banyak per pesanan.
   • Training & Support: GoFood tidak ada onboarding. DapurKu ada training
     gratis, packaging guidance, dan community manager di setiap kota.
   • Modal: GoFood butuh toko fisik. DapurKu zero modal — pakai dapur rumah.

   Source: Internal survey 50 sellers Batam pilot, 2026.

Q2: "Bagaimana kalau GoFood turun komisi jadi 15%?"
A: Moat kita bukan cuma harga. Tiga moat sustainable:
   • Community: Relationship dengan PKK, RT/RW, kelompok arisan.
     GoFood tidak punya community manager di kelurahan. Kita punya 1 per 50 sellers.
     Build time: 12-18 bulan. Replication cost: Rp 500M-1B per kota.
   • Local Knowledge: Menu lokal, bahan musiman, harga pasar.
     AI recommendation kita trained on local preferences.
   • Training Ecosystem: Bukan cuma platform, tapi entrepreneurship school.
     Sellers yang training 3x lebih likely survive year 1.

   Risk: Jika GoFood turun komisi + build community, threat level medium.
     Mitigation: Focus on tier 2-3 cities where GoFood less present.

Q3: "Bagaimana dengan food safety?"
A: Multi-layer protection:
   • Sertifikasi Dapur Bersih: Verifikasi video + random audit (30% sellers/month).
   • Insurance: Product liability Rp 100M per incident. Premium Rp 50jt/tahun.
   • Training: Hygiene gratis untuk semua sellers. Completion rate 85%.
   • BPOM/PIRT: Wajib untuk sellers dengan >100 orders/month.
     Kita bantu proses (biaya Rp 500K-2M per seller).
   • Buyer Protection: Refund full jika food poisoning terbukti.
     Incident rate target: <0.1% dari total orders.

   Legal: BPOM consultation on retainer. Health dept coordination per kota.

Q4: "Siapa yang antar makanan?"
A: Multi-partner strategy (asset-light):
   • Instant (30 min): Gojek/Grab integration untuk kota besar.
     Cost: Rp 8.000-12.000 per order (ke driver).
   • Scheduled (same day): Kurir komunitas (ibu-ibu yang punya motor).
     Cost: Rp 5.000-8.000 per order. Lower cost, community employment.
   • Pickup (zero cost): Buyer pickup di rumah seller.
     40% orders di Batam pilot = pickup (karena komunitas padat).
   • Kita tidak own fleet = zero capex delivery.
     Margin delivery: 0% (pass-through ke partner).

Q5: "Bagaimana penjual tidak kabur ke platform lain?"
A: Switching cost yang tinggi:
   • Data: History penjual, customer review, loyalty points.
     Rebuild di platform baru = 6-12 bulan.
   • Community: Seller group WhatsApp, offline events, mentorship.
     Emotional bond = "DapurKu adalah keluarga saya."
   • Financial: Weekly payment (bukan monthly seperti GoFood).
     Cash flow lebih baik = less incentive to switch.
   • Training Investment: 20+ jam training di DapurKu.
     Sunk cost = psychological barrier to switch.

   Data: Batam pilot M3 retention = 55% (industry average for food delivery = 40%).

Q6: "Unit economics-nya bagaimana?"
A: Dari Rp 40.000 pesanan (AOV):
   • Komisi DapurKu: Rp 4.800 (12%)
   • Payment gateway: Rp 400 (1%, ke Midtrans/Xendit)
   • Delivery partner: Rp 8.000 (20%, ke Gojek/Grab/kurir)
   • Net untuk penjual: Rp 26.800 (67%)

   Platform Economics:
   • Revenue per order: Rp 4.800 (commission) + Rp 1.200 (avg premium/ads) = Rp 6.000
   • CAC seller: Rp 25.000 (onboarding + training + first order subsidy)
   • CAC buyer: Rp 15.000 (digital ads + referral program)
   • LTV seller: Rp 180.000 (12 months x Rp 15.000 avg/month)
   • LTV buyer: Rp 120.000 (6 months x Rp 20.000 avg/month)
   • LTV/CAC: 7.2x seller, 8.0x buyer (healthy > 3x threshold)
   • Payback period: 4-5 months (conservative)

   Note: Unit economics based on Batam pilot (500 transactions, 50 sellers).
         Will improve with scale (lower CAC via word-of-mouth, higher LTV via retention).

Q7: "Break-even kapan?"
A: Conservative estimate:
   • Batam: Month 8-10 (target: 500 sellers, Rp 200M monthly GMV)
   • Jakarta: Month 10-12 (target: 1,000 sellers, Rp 500M monthly GMV)
   • Kota ke-3/4: Month 12-14 (template replication)
   • Nasional break-even: Year 4-5 (25 cities, 60,000 sellers)

   Assumptions:
   • Monthly burn rate: Rp 150-200M (seed phase)
   • Monthly revenue target: Rp 200M+ (Batam), Rp 500M+ (Jakarta)
   • Revenue = 14% take rate x GMV

   Risk: Break-even could extend 2-3 months if CAC higher than projected.
   Mitigation: Conservative burn rate, milestone-based spending.

Q10: "Siapa kompetitor terdekat?"
A: Direct competitors: None in Indonesia.
   Indirect competitors:
   • GoFood: 20-30% commission, target restoran/chain. Not for home cooks.
     Market share: 40% food delivery. Threat: High if they lower commission.
   • ShopeeFood: 15-25% commission, ecosystem play. Target chain restaurants.
     Market share: 25%. Threat: Medium (different segment).
   • GrabKitchen: 25% commission, cloud kitchen. Need Rp 10-20M setup.
     Market share: 15%. Threat: Low (different model).
   • Dahmakan (Malaysia): 15% commission, home kitchen. Not in Indonesia.
     Comparable model. Threat: Low (geographic).

   DapurKu differentiation: UMKM rumahan + community + zero capital.
   No direct competitor in Indonesia for this segment.

Q11: "Bagaimana skalabilitas ke kota kecil?"
A: Tier strategy (proven model):
   • Tier 1 (kota besar): Jakarta, Surabaya, Bandung. Digital adoption >80%.
     Strategy: Direct sales, digital marketing, Gojek/Grab integration.
   • Tier 2 (kota menengah): Semarang, Yogyakarta, Makassar. Digital 60-80%.
     Strategy: Hybrid (digital + community), local agent model.
   • Tier 3 (kota kecil): Agent model. 1 agent = 10-20 sellers.
     Pickup point di warung/toko. Scheduled delivery, not instant.
     Lower expectation, lower cost. Higher margin (less delivery subsidy).

   Expansion timeline: 2→4→8→15→25 cities over 5 years.
   Not all cities same strategy. Adapt per local condition.

Q12: "Regulasi bagaimana?"

   • Pre-launch (2026 Q2): PT registration, PSE (e-commerce), UU PDP,
     trademark, domain, BPOM/PIRT consultation.
   • Launch (2026 Q3): Halal certification (MUI), health dept coordination,
     seller KYC, payment gateway compliance (BI).
   • Scale (2027-2028): Multi-city health dept, ISO 27001, B2B partnerships.
   • Pre-IPO (2029-2030): Public company compliance, audit committee.

   Legal budget: Year 1 = Rp 150-200M (was Rp 50-100M, too low).
   Legal counsel: Retainer with reputable firm (Rp 30-50M/year).
   Government relations: Kemenkop, Dinas UMKM, BPOM.

Q13: "Bagaimana dengan delivery di daerah terpencil?"
A: Agent model for tier 3:
   • 1 agent = 10-20 sellers. Pickup point di warung/toko.
   • Scheduled delivery (2x per day), not instant.
   • Buyer pickup option (40% orders in Batam = pickup).
   • Lower cost: Rp 3.000-5.000 per order (vs Rp 8.000-12.000 instant).
   • Higher margin for platform: less delivery subsidy.

   Trade-off: Lower convenience = lower buyer expectation.
   But: Lower cost = higher seller margin = more sellers.

Q14: "Data privacy bagaimana?"
A: UU PDP compliant (Indonesia Personal Data Protection Law, Oct 2024):
   • Privacy policy: Bahasa Indonesia + English. Clear consent mechanism.
   • Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with sellers.
   • Data breach notification: 3x24 jam ke regulator + affected users.
   • Data Protection Officer (DPO): Required if >1000 users. We appoint at 500.
   • Encryption: AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit.
   • No data selling. Aggregated data only for market intelligence.
   • Annual security audit by third party.

   Cost: Rp 20-30M/year (DPO, audit, legal).

Q17: "Bagaimana kalau ekonomi turun?"
A: Defensive sector, but not recession-proof:
   • Positive: Home cooking = cheaper than restaurant. UMKM = more resilient.
     Orang mungkin order lebih dari home cooks saat ekonomi lesu.
   • Negative: Disposable income turun = less delivery orders.
     Premium features (ads, bundling) might decline.
   • Neutral: Core commission (12%) remains. Volume might increase (cheaper option).

   Historical data: Food delivery grew 15-20% during 2020-2021 recession
   (pandemic-driven, but shows resilience).

   Mitigation: Focus on affordable segment (Rp 20-40K AOV).
   Less exposure to premium dining.

Q21: "Ini bukan cuma marketplace biasa?"
A: Bukan. Tiga layer:
   • Layer 1 (Marketplace): Transaction = komisi 12%.
     Sama seperti GoFood/ShopeeFood.
   • Layer 2 (SaaS): Seller dashboard, analytics, inventory management.
     Premium = Rp 100K/bulan. Recurring revenue.
   • Layer 3 (Ecosystem): Training, financing, packaging supply.
     Community = emotional lock-in.

   Analogi: Amazon jual barang. Shopify bantu orang jualan.
   DapurKu = Shopify untuk makanan rumahan.
   Revenue mix: 60% commission, 20% premium, 15% ads, 5% supply.

Q22: "Bagaimana penjual tidak curang?"
A: Multi-layer protection (realistis, bukan over-confident):
   • Review system: Buyer rating + photo proof.
     Sellers with <3.5 rating = suspended.
   • Random audit: 10% orders per month = photo verification.
     Cost: Rp 5M/month untuk audit team.
   • Reputation score: Historical data, completion rate, complaint rate.
     Public = community pressure.
   • Insurance: Rp 100M coverage per incident.
     But: Fraud rate target <2% (realistis, bukan 0%).
   • Payment hold: 48-hour hold untuk new sellers (<100 orders).
     After 100 orders = instant payout.

   Note: Kita tidak claim "100% no fraud". Realistis: <2% fraud rate.

Q23: "Kenapa tidak cloud kitchen seperti GrabKitchen?"
A: Different model, different segment:
   • Cloud kitchen: Capex Rp 10-20M per kitchen. Butuh modal.
     Target: Brand besar, chain restaurants.
   • DapurKu: Zero capex. Pakai dapur rumah.
     Target: UMKM, home cooks, ibu rumah tangga.
   • Cloud kitchen = real estate play. DapurKu = platform play.
   • Cloud kitchen margin: 15-20%. DapurKu margin: 75% (platform).

   Future: Kita MUNGKIN build cloud kitchen untuk sellers yang scale up.
   But: Core model remains zero-capex for 90% of sellers.

Q24: "Bagaimana dengan margin tipis?"
A: Volume game + multiple revenue streams:
   • Micro-margin, macro-volume: 12% dari Rp 100T = Rp 12T.
     Walmart (2.4% margin), Amazon (2.4% margin), Gojek (5% margin).
   • Data monetization: Aggregated market intelligence.
     B2B: Food brands, FMCG companies. Rp 50-100M/year potential.
   • Premium services: Analytics, priority listing, financing.
     Higher margin (80%+) services.
   • Ads: Featured listing, banner. 80%+ margin.

   Long-term: Commission 60%, Premium 20%, Ads 15%, Data 5%.
   Blended margin: 75% gross, 10-15% net (Year 5).
