3. LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1x Non-Participating Preferred

Example:

- If exit at Rp 2B: Investor gets Rp 500M (1x preference) > Rp 400M (20% of 2B)

APPENDIX B: DEFINITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

"GMV" = Gross Merchandise Value = total transaction value on platform
"Active Seller" = seller with >= 1 transaction in past 30 days
"Active Buyer" = buyer with >= 1 transaction in past 30 days
"CAC" = Customer Acquisition Cost = total marketing spend / new customers
"LTV" = Lifetime Value = average revenue per customer x average lifespan
"Retention" = % of cohort users active at month N from month 1 cohort
"Burn Rate" = monthly net cash outflow (revenue - expenses)
"Runway" = cash balance / monthly burn rate
"Break-even" = monthly revenue >= monthly operating expenses
