FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

DapurKu Launches Ghost Kitchen Platform for UMKM Food Sellers in Indonesia

BATAM, Indonesia — [DATE] — DapurKu, a ghost kitchen platform dedicated to
empowering home-based food entrepreneurs, officially launched its pilot today
in Batam. The platform enables home cooks across Indonesia to sell their food
online without capital investment, charging 10-15% commission compared to the
industry standard of 20-30%.

THE PROBLEM

Despite Indonesia's growing food delivery market (estimated Rp 100 trillion
by 2026, source: RedSeer/Google Report 2025), the UMKM segment remains
underserved. Existing food delivery services target established restaurants
with high commissions, leaving home cooks without viable options.

"50% of UMKM food businesses struggle in the first year," said [Founder Name],
CEO and Founder of DapurKu. "Not because they can't cook, but because they
don't have access to affordable digital platforms."
(Source: Kemenkop UKM, 2024 Report)

THE SOLUTION

DapurKu provides a zero-capital platform specifically designed for UMKM food
sellers. Key features include:

• Zero capital requirement — sellers use their existing home kitchen
• 10-15% commission — 40-50% lower than competitors
• Community support — training, packaging guidance, marketing assistance
• Pre-order system — cook-to-order, reducing food waste
• Verified kitchen certification — building buyer trust through video verification
• Weekly bundling — subscription model for recurring revenue

TRACTION

During its one-month pilot in Batam, DapurKu achieved:
• 50 active sellers onboarded
• 500 transactions completed
• 4.7 out of 5 average rating
• Projected 55% seller retention at month 3 (validation ongoing)

Note: Retention data is projected based on early signals. Full validation
requires 3-6 months of data.

"We built DapurKu because we believe every home cook deserves the opportunity
to become an entrepreneur," said [Founder Name]. "Our mothers, sisters, and
neighbors cook amazing food. They just need a platform that understands them."

MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Indonesia's food delivery market is growing rapidly, but the UMKM segment
remains underserved. DapurKu targets the Rp 20 trillion SAM (Serviceable
Addressable Market) with plans to expand to 25 cities by 2030, serving
60,000 sellers and generating Rp 5 trillion in GMV.

Note: TAM = Rp 100 trillion (food delivery), SAM = Rp 20 trillion (UMKM
segment), SOM = Rp 500 billion (0.5% penetrasi 5 tahun).
