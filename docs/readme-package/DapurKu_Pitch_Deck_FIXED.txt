SLIDE 6: BUSINESS MODEL (REVISED - UNIT ECONOMICS)
──────────────
Revenue Streams:
• Komisi transaksi: 12% per pesanan (60% revenue)
• Premium penjual: Rp 100K/bulan untuk analytics & priority (20% revenue)
• Iklan: Featured listing, banner dari brand (15% revenue)
• Kemasan & supply: Packaging, bahan, equipment (5% revenue)

Unit Economics (per order Rp 40K):
• Komisi: Rp 4.800 (12%)
• Payment: Rp 400 (1%, ke Midtrans)
• Delivery: Rp 8.000 (20%, ke partner)
• Net penjual: Rp 26.800 (67%)

Platform Economics:
• CAC seller: Rp 25.000
• LTV seller: Rp 180.000
• LTV/CAC: 7.2x (healthy > 3x)
• Payback: 4-5 months
