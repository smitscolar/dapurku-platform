MARKET ASSUMPTIONS:
• Total Population Indonesia: 278.8 juta (2026 BPS)
• Target Penetrasi Year 5: 0.5% (realistis, bukan 2%)
• Average Order Value (AOV): Rp 40,000 (konservatif)
• Transaction Frequency: 2x per minggu (realistis, bukan 3x)
• Commission Rate: 12% (base)
• Premium Rate: 3% (untuk seller tier 2+)
• Ads Rate: 2% (untuk featured listing)
• Total Take Rate: 17% (maksimum, rata-rata 14%)

GROWTH ASSUMPTIONS (REVISED - REALISTIS):
• Year 1 (2026): 2 kota (Batam, Jakarta) = 500 sellers
• Year 2 (2027): 4 kota (+Bandung, Surabaya) = 2,000 sellers  
• Year 3 (2028): 8 kota (+Semarang, Yogyakarta, Banten, Bali) = 8,000 sellers
• Year 4 (2029): 15 kota (tier 2 expansion) = 25,000 sellers
• Year 5 (2030): 25 kota (nasional) = 60,000 sellers

UNIT ECONOMICS (REVISED):
• CAC (Seller): Rp 25,000 (realistis untuk onboarding + training)
• CAC (Buyer): Rp 15,000 (realistis untuk digital marketing)
• LTV (Seller): Rp 180,000 (12 bulan x Rp 15,000 avg commission/month)
• LTV (Buyer): Rp 120,000 (6 bulan x Rp 20,000 avg spend/month)
• LTV/CAC Ratio: 7.2x seller, 8x buyer (healthy > 3x)
• Seller Retention M3: 55% (realistis, bukan 60%)
• Buyer Retention M3: 35% (realistis, bukan 40%)

COST ASSUMPTIONS (REVISED):
• Monthly Burn Rate (Seed): Rp 150-200 juta (include payroll)
• Monthly Burn Rate (Series A): Rp 400-600 juta
• Monthly Burn Rate (Series B): Rp 1.5-2.5 miliar
• Gross Margin: 75% (platform business, tidak 85%)
• Operating Margin Target Y5: 10-15%
